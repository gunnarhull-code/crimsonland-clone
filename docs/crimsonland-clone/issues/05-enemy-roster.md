## Question

What enemy roster does the MVP ship with?

## Answer

**Three base species, each with a "big/boss" variant** (six total behavior profiles):

- **Spider** — wander phase uses circular/figure-eight-style variants; chase style is a burst "charge-stop-charge" pattern once aggroed.
- **Rat** — fast, small; tight/erratic wander variants; low HP; a swarm-style threat.
- **Alien** — slower; looser/wider wander variants; higher HP; steadier straight-line charge once aggroed (the "brute" role).

Each species' big/boss variant shares the same wander/chase pattern as its base form but moves **slower** — no separate behavior design needed per variant, just a speed/scale/HP modifier.

Exact wander-variant definitions, timers, and chase-parameter numbers are deferred to [10 - enemy AI parameters](10-enemy-ai-parameters.md).
