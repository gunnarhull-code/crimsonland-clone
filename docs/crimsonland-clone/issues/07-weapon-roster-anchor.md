## Question

Which weapon archetypes anchor the MVP's roster, and how many weapons total?

## Answer

**5-6 weapons total** (vs. the original's ~25-30 — enough variety to prove speed/accuracy/fire-rate/reload differences matter, without needing to balance dozens of entries).

Confirmed anchor weapons:
- **Pistol** — the starting weapon (see [04](04-weapon-pickup-ammo-model.md)).
- **A piercing Gauss-style gun** — bullets pass through multiple enemies. This matches the original exactly: piercing is a dedicated weapon trait there (the Gauss Gun/Gauss Shotgun family), **not a perk** — the MVP follows suit rather than making piercing an equippable perk.
- **An electric/chain weapon** — its shot arcs to hit one additional nearby enemy beyond its primary target.

The remaining 2-3 slots, and the full numeric stat table for every weapon in the roster (fire rate, magazine size, reload time, projectile speed, accuracy/spread, damage, and the electric weapon's chain range/target count), are deferred to [11 - weapon stat table](11-weapon-stat-table.md).
