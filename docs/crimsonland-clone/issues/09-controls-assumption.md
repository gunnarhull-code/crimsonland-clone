## Question

What is the base control scheme? (Not explicitly asked in the interview — recorded as an assumption per the user's standing instruction to default to the original wherever confident.)

## Answer

**WASD for movement, mouse to aim, left-click (held) to fire** — matches the original exactly (research doc, section 2). Movement and aim are fully independent: the player can strafe/move in any direction while aiming anywhere else (a twin-stick feel, even though aim is mouse-driven rather than a second stick).
